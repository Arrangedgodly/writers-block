### Design-specificity verdict
AUTHORED, not category-interchangeable — for four of five phases. The write/outcome/archive surfaces are a genuinely specific instrument world: recessed 1ch×1em flap wells with a 1px hinge seam, unlit lamps kept as dark tinted glass (absence as information), caution amber confined to a constant-width 2px leading-edge band while the field stays achromatic, DSEG14 reserved for SIGNAL LOST alone, Zulu timestamps as a right-margin numeric spine, prose measure held at 70ch. No unrelated product could ship this unchanged. The refusal list (serene writing canvas, glassy dashboard, neon terminal) is honored structurally.

The specificity HOLE is the setup phase: the first viewport a visitor sees is an unstyled semantic form (visible h1 in prose face, two bare fieldsets, UA-default radios, native number input, two class-less buttons ARM / VIEW ARCHIVE) on the charcoal ground — precisely the "stock web furniture" the system refuses. The direction contract's FIRST VIEWPORT promise (split-flap duration board presiding; GENTLE/STANDARD/BRUTAL placards printing FADE/LOSS limits; guarded ARM isolated low) is not built. KNOWN FINDING CONFIRMED at source: router.ts `renderSetup()` emits `.placard`, `.placard-name`, `.placard-limits`, `data-phase="setup"`; no sheet in main.css imports consumes them; ARM/VIEW ARCHIVE carry no plate classes; `--dur-arm` (260ms) sits unused in tokens.css. Material gap (P1).

### Cognitive load
One boundary failure: the setup duration group presents 5 visible options (3/5/10/15 + CUSTOM), pushing the ≤4 working-memory boundary at the product's first decision. All other checklist items pass (write-phase single focus exemplary; limits printed; progressive disclosure via VIEW reveal and in-flow confirm). LOW load overall.

### Emotional journey (peak-end)
Peak: the fade (amber band + paling text + ticking board) and the deletion beat (three-step annunciator latch, emptied console, honest copy, focused RE-ARM) are architecturally strong. End: the archive payoff with the finalized entry's VIEW focused is the right mercy-after-mechanism. Valley: the ENTRY — first emotional contact is a default OS form; and the restart loop re-enters that valley at the lowest emotional moment (post-deletion), doubling the cost of the gap.

### Strengths
1. The write surface is the thesis executed: presiding tabular board in fixed wells (zero layout shift by construction), threat as a constant-width 2px band + engine-driven per-frame opacity, no live stats, 70ch prose. Nothing between the writer and words.
2. Accessibility as architecture: persistent status/alert regions created before first paint, once-per-onset fade announcements, milestone role promotion, per-phase h1s, deliberate focus map on every swap, textContent-only rendering, computed contrast ≥4.5:1 (T12 record). The fade is never opacity-alone as a signal.
3. One console, one vocabulary across phases: same plates/lamps/wells; unlit lamps stay present; every failure message names the next move.
